Ask your providerConfirm compatibility, costs and ongoing support.

Compatibility depends on the existing lock, door dimensions and hardware. A provider should check the door before recommending equipment.

Fallback options vary. Ask about low-battery warnings, physical keys and emergency power before selecting a device.

Many models support scheduled or temporary codes. Confirm the available controls and whether they depend on a subscription.

Some smart locks retain a key cylinder; others use different fallback methods. Make this preference clear in your brief.
