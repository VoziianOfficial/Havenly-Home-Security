What this website stores

This website stores your privacy choice in your browser’s local storage under the key havenly-consent. It is used to remember your choice on this device.

Essential functionality

Navigation, sliders, accordions and the enquiry form do not require advertising cookies. This website has no added analytics or marketing trackers.

External resources

Fonts may be loaded from external services. Photos and interface libraries are served with this website. Your browser sends the information needed to request those assets. These requests are separate from our locally stored preference.

Storage on shared devices

The preference is stored in the current browser. Another browser, a private browsing session or a different device may show the preference card again. Clearing site data also removes the stored choice.

What the controls do

Accept and Essential only record your preference without changing the available navigation or enquiry features. Cookie settings reopens the card so you can choose again. No advertising consent is used to activate hidden marketing scripts.

External links

Links to credits and other websites take you to services with their own storage practices. Review their information if you choose to visit them.

Managing your preference

Use Cookie settings in the footer to reopen the privacy card. You can also clear website data through your browser settings. Accept and Essential only currently enable the same necessary website functions.

Questions

Contact hello@havenly.example if you have questions about storage on this website.
