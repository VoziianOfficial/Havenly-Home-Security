About the platform

Havenly offers general information and an enquiry route for people exploring home security. The platform does not supply, install, monitor or maintain security equipment.

Information and suitability

Content is a starting point for your own enquiries. Equipment compatibility, coverage and suitability depend on your property and should be assessed by the provider you choose.

Enquiries and introductions

Submitting a form does not create a service agreement or guarantee a provider match, response time or availability. You are not required to accept any proposal.

Independent providers

Any quote, installation, monitoring agreement, warranty or payment arrangement is between you and the chosen provider. Review their scope, credentials, terms, charges and support arrangements before proceeding.

Comparing proposals

Ask a provider to separate equipment costs, installation work, recurring subscriptions and optional support. Confirm what is included and retain a copy of the agreed proposal.

Planning illustrations

Interactive camera frames, recording choices and other visual controls are planning aids. They do not operate equipment, measure real coverage or verify that a particular system is suitable for your property.

Illustrative prices

Example plans show how monthly costs may be presented. They are not binding quotations. A provider must confirm the actual equipment, installation and recurring charges before any purchase.

Responsible use

Do not misuse the form, submit unlawful material or attempt to gain unauthorised access to the website. Use accurate contact details and avoid sharing confidential security information.

Availability and changes

Content and available enquiry options may change. The website may be temporarily unavailable for maintenance or other operational reasons. Nothing here is intended to remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
